The **source code** of this project is under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). The **data** it
gathers, in the bundled database release, the offline map layers in `app/static/data/` and the
figures in `docs/img/`, comes from the sources below and stays under their licences. If you reuse
the data, keep these credits, and note the two share-alike licences:

- Text taken from **Wikipedia** is under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Anything you make from it must credit
  Wikipedia and be shared under the same licence.
- Map data derived from **OpenStreetMap** is under the **Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0**. A
  database you make from it must also be offered under the ODbL.

Nothing here is legal advice; follow each source's own terms.

## Change over time (graphs on the Map page and the Change over time page)
Series and their sources are listed in `data/curated/change/sources.json` (with download URLs, licence quotes and the
date each was checked) and on the page itself; dated events in `data/curated/change/events.json` each carry their
citation. Only openly licensed series are shown. Left out for their licence: Bank of England "A millennium of
macroeconomic data" (Broadberry et al. population and wages; terms limited to personal and internal use), Armagh
Observatory temperatures (copyright retained), SILSO sunspot numbers and the Shennan et al. sea-level database (non-commercial).

Authority (Coal Authority) Coalfield Consultation Areas, OGL v3.0.
