Research checked against its sources

Every history event and every researched "oldest of its type" claim was checked automatically: the cited source was fetched and searched for the stated year and name. "Year not found" does not mean the claim is wrong (the source may give it in words or as a century) but those entries deserve a manual look.

Researched claims

Claims researched in original and published sources (data/curated/evidence.json): each source with its kind, where in it, and an exact quote that is looked for in the source again (python -m bidb.evidence --check). The quotes are checked automatically; the judgements are not.
