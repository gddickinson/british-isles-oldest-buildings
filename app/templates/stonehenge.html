Stonehenge in 3D

About the model

As it is now: every surviving stone from Anthony Johnson's numbered plan of the stones as they survive, with the height recorded for each standing stone; lintels still in place sit on their uprights; the Heel Stone leans as it does. As built: the sarsen circle of 30 uprights and 30 curved lintels, the five trilithons (stones 55 and 59 set where the horseshoe's symmetry puts them; the surviving pairs agree with it within half a metre), the bluestone circle and horseshoe filled in at the spacing of the stones that survive, the Altar Stone standing on the axis (its first position is not known), the Slaughter Stone and the Heel Stone upright with its lost companion, and the four Station Stones. "Mark lost stones" draws the lost ones pale: they are placed where they are thought to have stood; the bluestones are shown in their last arrangement (c. 2400–2200 BC).

Click a stone for its record and the sources quoted for it. The ground is the Environment Agency's 1 m LiDAR survey (the henge bank and ditch, the Avenue's banks and the barrows show in it); beyond it the land rises to the real skyline, so the sun and moon rise and set where they would. Sun, moon and stars are placed for the date and time chosen (local solar time; years before AD 1582 in the Gregorian calendar extended back). The moon before about 500 BC is uncertain by up to half a degree, since the earth's rotation then (ΔT) is known only to about an hour. Planets are not shown.
